## What we are proposing

A connected marketing system that helps Come Again **create content faster**, **reach more travellers online**, **capture more inquiries**, and **follow up automatically** — across your website, email, and social media.

AI will assist with writing and planning. **Your team always reviews and approves** everything before it goes live. Nothing is published without your say-so.

---

## The problem today

Your website looks professional and your packages are strong, but marketing is still largely manual:

- Blog and social content take time to write
- Newsletter signup on the site does not yet collect emails
- Inquiries come in by email only — there is no central lead list
- Social media is posted manually with no content calendar
- We cannot easily see which pages or posts bring the most bookings

---

## Our approach

We build on what you already have — your website and contact form — and add a marketing layer behind the scenes.

```
Your website  →  Marketing hub  →  Email & social channels
                      ↑
                 AI assists here
              (drafts for your approval)
```

### Four pillars

| Pillar | What it means for Come Again |
|--------|------------------------------|
| **Create** | AI drafts blog posts, social captions, and emails based on your safari packages |
| **Publish** | Approved content goes to your website, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and email list |
| **Capture** | Every inquiry and newsletter signup is saved in one place |
| **Learn** | Track which packages and posts drive the most interest |

## What gets delivered

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–6)

*Get the basics working.*

- Working newsletter signup on the website
- Central lead database (all contact form inquiries stored and searchable)
- Full blog with readable articles (not just a list of titles)
- Better website tracking (see which packages people view and where inquiries come from)
- Simple admin panel for your team to view leads and subscribers

**Result:** Your website becomes a proper lead-generation tool.

---

### Phase 2 — AI content & email (Weeks 7–14)

*Save time on content and stay in touch with prospects.*

- AI content assistant — generates blog posts, social captions, and email copy from your package information
- Approval workflow — your team reviews, edits, and approves before anything is published
- Email marketing connected (welcome emails for new subscribers, seasonal safari promotions)
- Campaign landing pages for special offers (e.g. gorilla trekking season, migration safaris)

**Result:** Consistent content output without hiring a full-time copywriter.

---

### Phase 3 — Social automation & insights (Weeks 15–22)

*Connect everything and measure what works.*

- Approved social posts scheduled to Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn
- One blog post automatically turned into multiple social posts and an email summary
- Performance dashboard — traffic, leads, newsletter growth, top packages
- Optional: website chat to answer common safari questions and guide visitors to contact you

**Result:** A marketing engine that runs with less manual effort and clearer results.

## How AI fits in (simply)

AI is a **writing and planning assistant**, not a replacement for your team.

**AI will:**
- Draft a blog post about a new safari package
- Suggest Instagram captions and hashtags
- Write a welcome email for new subscribers
- Propose a monthly social media content calendar

**Your team will:**
- Check facts (prices, park fees, visa info)
- Edit tone and wording
- Approve before publishing
- Handle real customer conversations and bookings

This keeps Come Again's voice authentic and accurate.
